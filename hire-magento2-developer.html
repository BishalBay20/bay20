FAQs

Questions we hear before every Magento 2 hire.

Everything merchants ask us about Magento 2 development, migration, customisation and the support that follows. If your question is not here, ask us directly.

Ask us a question
What benefits do Magento 2 certified developers and designers bring to my business?
Magento 2 certified developers and designers bring expertise in optimising your online store's performance, enhancing user experience, implementing secure payment gateways, customising themes and ensuring smooth integration with third-party systems.
What is Magento eCommerce development?
Magento eCommerce development is the process of creating and customising online stores using the Magento platform. It involves designing and developing the website, integrating features and functionalities, managing products and optimising the user experience for seamless online shopping.
Which languages are used in Magento development?
Magento development primarily uses PHP, the core programming language of the platform. Developers also use HTML, CSS, JavaScript, XML and MySQL for front-end design, styling, interactivity, data management and database integration.
Can Bay20 migrate my website from another CMS to Magento?
Yes. Bay20 specialises in website migration services, including migrating websites from other content management systems to Magento, as well as Magento 1 to Magento 2 upgrades with products, customers, orders and SEO data preserved.
How long does it take to develop a Magento web store from scratch?
The timeline varies with the complexity and specific requirements of the project. Design complexity, feature integrations, customisations and data migration all influence the development duration. We give you a realistic estimate after a short discovery call.
Can your developers and designers customise themes and templates in Magento 2?
Absolutely. Our Magento 2 certified professionals are proficient in customising themes and templates to create a unique and visually appealing online store that matches your brand.
Do you offer ongoing support and maintenance after the project is completed?
Yes. We provide ongoing support and maintenance services for your Magento 2 website even after the project is completed, including security patches, version upgrades and performance monitoring.
Can your developers integrate third-party extensions or APIs with Magento 2?
Absolutely. Our Magento 2 certified developers have expertise in integrating third-party extensions and APIs, such as ERP, CRM, payment, shipping and marketing tools, seamlessly with your Magento 2 website.
Can your developers optimise the performance of my Magento 2 website?
Certainly. Our developers conduct performance audits, identify and resolve bottlenecks, optimise code and implement caching techniques such as Varnish and Redis to make your store faster.
Are your Magento 2 certified developers and designers familiar with responsive design practices?
Yes. Our Magento 2 certified professionals are well-versed in responsive design practices and ensure your store provides a seamless user experience across desktops, tablets and phones.
