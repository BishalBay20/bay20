Bay20 · Visual direction 01

Crimson & Ink.

A modern palette built from the existing Bay20 logo (crimson + black) and deliberately moved away from the warm-cream, terracotta look of the reference site. Red stays the single call-to-action colour, near-black carries the hero and “proof” sections, and a cool glacier tint is reserved for technology and platform content so the page never reads as all-red.
